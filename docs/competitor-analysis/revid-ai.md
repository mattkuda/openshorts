## What they do

Revid.ai (originally Typeframes.com) is a high-volume AI video generator whose tagline is **"Create viral videos in Minutes."** You give it an idea — a topic, a written script, a URL/article, a Reddit post, an audio file, a YouTube link, or your own uploaded clip — and it auto-produces a finished 9:16 video with AI visuals (stock footage, AI images, or AI video), an AI voiceover (50+ voices, 30+ languages / 68 countries), captions, and platform-ready pacing. The pitch is that it does scripting, voiceover, visuals, and subtitles automatically so a non-editor can ship daily.

The product is positioned squarely at **faceless channel operators and short-form marketers** who want volume. Beyond one-off generation, it offers an **Auto-Mode** with "workers" that automatically produce videos on a schedule (e.g. one per day) from a content source, and can **auto-publish** to TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. It markets a large surface area of "tools" (claims range from ~42 to 100+ specialized tools and 100+ templates) covering AI talking avatars, face swap, deepfake/clone effects, music videos, PDF/article/website-to-video, and more.

It self-reports strong traction (14,258+ creators, 240,909+ videos created, 400+ creators with 100k+ views) and emphasizes that output is watermark-free and "100% yours" with commercial-use rights.

Two of its flows matter most for OpenShorts: (1) an **AI video face swap** that drops a new face onto an existing performance, and (2) a **post-generation editor** built around editing the video as text.

## Core workflow

1. Click **Create New Video** in the left sidebar.
2. **Pick a video type** — Short Video Generator, AI Talking Avatar, Article-to-Video, Audio-to-Video, YouTube-Long-to-Shorts, etc.
3. **Provide input** — type/paste a script, paste a URL, upload audio/video, paste a YouTube link, or hit "Script Generator" for AI to write it.
4. **Configure** — voice (or record yourself), background music (or AI-generate it), media type (stock / AI image / AI video), aspect ratio (9:16, 16:9, 1:1), caption preset (BASIC, REVID, HORMOZY, etc.), plus "More options."
5. **Generate video** (shows estimated credit cost, e.g. "10 credits").
6. **Edit** in the editor — edit the transcript text (which edits the video), adjust per-segment timing/duration, regenerate or replace media per frame, split frames, change caption styling/position, set safe zones.
7. **Publish** directly to TikTok / Instagram / YouTube, **Export/download**, or hand off to **Auto-Mode** for scheduled, recurring, auto-published videos.

## Key features

- **Faceless AI video generator** — topic/script/URL → finished vertical video with AI visuals, voiceover, captions.
- **AI video face swap** — upload a source video + one front-facing reference photo; AI maps the new face frame-by-frame while preserving "head movement, timing, and facial performance," expressions/body language, and the original audio/voice/lighting (voice replacement optional). Marketed for short-form, marketing/spokesperson variations, parody, roleplay.
- **Clone Multiplier & deepfake/talking-avatar tools** — viral "clone" effects from an uploaded reference image; AI talking avatars / "AI Characters (beta)" as on-screen presenters.
- **Smart Transcript Editor** — "Edit by typing, not clicking" / "edit like a document": delete a sentence → deletes that segment, move text blocks → reorder scenes, delete words → cut scenes; auto-removes filler words ("um," "uh," pauses).
- **Media Editor** — drag-and-drop scene controls, one-click visual replacement/regeneration, per-frame media, frame splitting, aspect-ratio switcher, safe zones.
- **Caption editor** — auto captions ("99% accurate," 100+ languages), edit text directly in transcript view, per-segment duration editing, font/color/position styling, SRT export or burn-in.
- **Voices & languages** — 50+ AI voices, 30+ languages / 68 countries; voice cloning on Elite+.
- **Multi-input converters** — article/website-to-video, PDF-to-video, Reddit-post-to-video, audio/podcast-to-video, YouTube-long-to-shorts, text-to-video.
- **Repurpose / viral discovery** — surfaces "viral posts from your topics across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok" to remix; markets that it "analyzes viral videos and uses those same codes to write your scripts."
- **Auto-Mode workers** — always-on daily video pipelines (3 standard / 5 Elite / 10 Ultra) with optional auto-publishing.
- **One-click multi-platform publishing**, watermark-free export, up to 4K, API/MCP/CLI access.

## Output / content types

- Faceless short-form vertical videos (9:16) for TikTok, Reels, Shorts — also 16:9 and 1:1.
- AI talking-avatar / "AI presenter" videos (face shown without being your real face).
- Face-swapped videos (your/another face onto an existing clip).
- "Clone multiplier" / deepfake viral-effect videos.
- AI music videos synced to audio.
- Article / website / PDF / Reddit / podcast-audio → video.
- Repurposed long YouTube → shorts; remixed versions of trending posts.
- Scheduled, auto-published recurring videos via Auto-Mode workers.

## Strengths

- **Breadth + volume**: huge tool/template surface and Auto-Mode workers make daily faceless-channel automation genuinely turnkey.
- **Text-first editing**: the Smart Transcript Editor ("edit like a document," delete-sentence-deletes-segment) is a fast, low-skill way to fine-tune output — exactly the pattern OpenShorts wants.
- **Beginner-friendly**: reviewers note most users "master the basics in less than 5 minutes."
- **End-to-end**: ideation → generation → edit → schedule → auto-publish in one tool, no external scheduler needed.
- **Watermark-free, commercial rights, 4K**, broad language/voice coverage, and API/MCP/CLI for power users.

## Weaknesses & gaps

- **Credit anxiety**: everything (images, clips, voice, avatars, 4K export) burns credits; AI-video clips cost 15–200 credits each and a 30s Pro video runs ~40–80 credits, so heavy/long use gets expensive fast and forces upsell to higher tiers or boost packs.
- **Generic AI-video look**: stock/AI-image-driven faceless output is commoditized; quality on cheaper models/credits is uneven, and the "faceless" aesthetic is what every competitor also ships.
- **Face swap is upload-only**: it requires a directly uploaded source video + reference photo — there is **no "paste a viral video URL and clone it"** path in the face-swap tool itself (link-based cloning is adjacent third-party tools like VidCloner, not Revid's swap flow). The "repurpose" tab is a discovery-and-remix feed, not a literal one-link clone of a specific video.
- **No true free plan** and pricing/plan names drift across sources (Hobby/Growth/Ultra vs Lite/Growth/Elite/Ultra; $29 vs $32 vs $39), suggesting frequent repackaging.
- **Jack-of-all-trades sprawl**: 100+ tools dilute focus; none is a deep, defensible UGC-ad workflow.

## Relevant to OpenShorts (steal / avoid)

- **Steal:** The **post-generation editor pattern for in-app text editing** — Revid's Smart Transcript Editor ("Edit by typing, not clicking," delete a sentence to cut that segment, edit captions inline, edit per-segment duration). This "edit the video by editing its text" model is precisely the in-app text-editing step OpenShorts wants to copy: render first, then let the user fine-tune timing/captions/visuals without a timeline.
- **Avoid / their gap we exploit:** **Face swap / clone-a-viral-video is table stakes, not a differentiator.** Revid (plus VidCloner, CloneViral, Pollo, etc.) all offer "drop your face onto a proven viral clip." OpenShorts should treat replicate-a-viral-video as a checkbox feature, not a moat. Also note Revid's face swap is upload-only and its "clone" is really discovery+remix — neither is a clean "paste this exact viral link → my-face version." The real opening is a focused, high-quality **UGC ad** workflow (authentic-feeling creator ads with a tight, editable pipeline) rather than another sprawling faceless-video tool buried in credit math.
