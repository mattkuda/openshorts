## What they do

ReelFarm is an AI TikTok-automation platform built around the promise "Automate TikToks that drive traffic to your website" and "use AI to generate TikTok videos that don't feel like AI." It is a bootstrapped product founded in August 2024 by Matt Welter (a full-stack engineer based in Arizona) and, per its marketing, has published 100,000+ posts for 5,000+ customers. The core pitch is removing humans from the content pipeline entirely: you describe your business/niche once, set up an "automation," and ReelFarm generates and auto-publishes faceless short-form content on a recurring schedule.

The product spans four content types — TikTok image **Slideshows** (its flagship listicle/carousel format), **Hook + Demo** AI-UGC product videos, **Greenscreen Meme** videos, and **AI UGC Selfies** (avatar images/videos) — all oriented toward TikTok virality and traffic. It also bundles supporting tooling: an AI **Hook Generator** (enter brand + 40+ char product description, get viral opening lines in ~4–7s), a **UGC Avatar Generator** that creates custom AI characters from a plain-text description (e.g. "bald guy wearing a suit"), and **automatic image sourcing** that pulls relevant imagery from Pinterest by niche so the user never uploads assets.

A notable free, top-of-funnel asset is the **Slideshow Library**: a searchable inspiration database of real slideshows that businesses have posted, browsable by niche and sortable by view count ("most viewed") so users can reverse-engineer what's working. It's accessible without payment and functions as both an inspiration layer and a lead magnet.

Distribution is a first-class part of the product (unlike OpenShorts V1): ReelFarm connects to TikTok via official login, supports "warming" new accounts with a few days of organic engagement, manages multiple TikTok accounts, and includes a built-in scheduler that publishes directly to TikTok. The whole thing is framed as "set up once, and it runs forever."

## Core workflow

1. **Connect & warm a TikTok account** — log in via TikTok's official auth; new/cold accounts are warmed for ~3–5 days of organic engagement before posting.
2. **Describe the business/niche** — enter brand + a product description (40+ characters); this seeds hooks, slideshow text, and image sourcing.
3. **Pick a content type** — Slideshow, Hook + Demo, Greenscreen Meme, or AI UGC Selfie.
4. **Generate a hook** — use the AI Hook Generator to produce/save/refresh viral opening lines (listicle patterns like "5 habits…", "3 foods that…").
5. **(Optional) Generate or pick an avatar** — describe a custom AI character in text ("bald guy wearing a suit") to get a reusable persona, or use a preset avatar (custom avatars are gated to Growth+).
6. **Assemble** — for slideshows, ReelFarm splits text into slides, auto-sources imagery from Pinterest, and adds captions, transitions, and background music; for UGC, it places the avatar + your product video/image.
7. **Edit in the real-time editor** — light tweaks to text/preset styling.
8. **Schedule or one-click publish to TikTok** — or hand it to an "automation" that creates and posts content daily on autopilot.
9. **Track analytics** — views/likes/shares per post.
10. **(Browse anytime) Slideshow Library** — search competitor/peer slideshows by niche, sort by most-viewed, and copy what works.

## Key features

- Four content types: TikTok Slideshows, Hook + Demo AI-UGC videos, Greenscreen Meme videos, AI UGC Selfies (images + videos).
- **Custom AI avatar generator from a text description** ("bald guy wearing a suit") → reusable brand persona; reviewers report generating one character ("Sam") across dozens of outfits/settings for brand recognition.
- **AI Hook Generator** — viral opening lines from brand + product description in seconds; save/edit/refresh.
- **Free Slideshow Library** — searchable inspiration DB of real posts, filterable by niche, sortable by view count.
- **Automatic Pinterest image sourcing** by niche — no manual asset uploads.
- **Automations / campaigns** — "set up once, runs forever," generating + posting on a daily schedule around a niche.
- **Built-in scheduler + direct TikTok publishing**; multi-account management and account warming.
- Real-time slideshow editor (preset-level control).
- Team collaboration (unlimited members on paid tiers) and unlimited TikTok accounts (Growth+).
- **API access + a `Skill.md`** (agent/automation hook) on all tiers; **BYO Anthropic API key** on Unlimited for uncapped generations.

## Output / content types

- **TikTok Slideshows / image carousels** — flagship; listicle/meme-styled bold on-screen text, auto transitions + music, Pinterest-sourced imagery.
- **Hook + Demo videos** — AI-UGC avatar delivers a hook, then a product demo (your b-roll/video/image).
- **Greenscreen Meme videos** — AI character over custom backgrounds + text, relatable to the product/business.
- **AI UGC Selfies** — avatar selfie images and videos for native-feeling UGC.
- All optimized 9:16 for TikTok specifically.

## Strengths

- **True end-to-end automation** — generation + scheduling + auto-publishing in one loop; genuinely "hands-off" after setup. Reviewers report real volume (e.g., 112 videos / ~412k views over 90 days).
- **Strong, differentiated inspiration layer** — the free, searchable, view-count-ranked Slideshow Library is a real "proven formats" data asset and a smart lead magnet.
- **Reusable-character workflow that works in practice** — one custom avatar across many looks builds brand recognition; reviewers found custom-avatar + owned b-roll outperformed stock-footage variants.
- **No manual asset hunting** — auto image sourcing removes the biggest friction in slideshow creation.
- **Cheap, flat, legible pricing** vs. UGC agencies/creators; transparent tiers.
- **Multi-account + team + API/Skill.md** make it credible for agencies and power users running many accounts.

## Weaknesses & gaps

- **TikTok-only.** No native Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or Facebook publishing; cross-platform repurposing is "manual, time-consuming." (The official guides/marketing lean entirely TikTok.)
- **Shallow editing.** Preset/template-driven; reviewers note you "cannot fine-tune visuals, timing, or text style beyond basic presets." No deep, direct text-on-reel editing.
- **Stock-y filler.** Auto-sourced demo/stock imagery "screams stock footage" vs. branded b-roll.
- **Key features paywalled & capped** — custom avatars are Growth+ only, and avatar/image generation is capped even on higher tiers (credit-metered).
- **Account-warming learning curve** and TikTok-platform-risk exposure (mass faceless automation).
- **No standalone "paste a viral link → replicate format"** automation surfaced — inspiration is browse-and-copy via the Library, not one-click cloning of an arbitrary URL.
- **No campaign-variation matrix** — automations repeat a format on a schedule rather than fanning out a batch of hook/CTA/actor/background permutations for A/B testing.
- Thin independent/verified user-review base; most coverage is affiliate/SEO content.

## Relevant to OpenShorts (steal / avoid)

- **Steal:**
  - **The free, searchable, view-ranked Slideshow Library** is almost exactly our "proven formats" data layer. Ship our version as a public, niche-filtered, sort-by-views inspiration DB — it doubles as top-of-funnel SEO/lead-gen.
  - **Text-to-avatar character creation** ("bald guy wearing a suit") plus the **"one character, many looks"** reuse pattern — validated by a reviewer running a single persona ("Sam") across dozens of looks. This is core to our vision; ReelFarm proves users want a reusable, consistent brand character.
  - **The four-content-type framing** (Slideshow / Hook+Demo / Greenscreen Meme / AI-UGC Selfie) is a clean, market-tested taxonomy for our UGC modes — and **listicle/TikTok-slideshow** generation maps directly to our slideshow + listicle vision.
  - **Hook generator from a short brand description** with save/edit/refresh — cheap, fast, high-perceived-value onboarding step.
  - **`Skill.md` + API** as an explicit agent/automation surface is a clever positioning detail worth mirroring.
- **Avoid / their gap we exploit:**
  - **They're TikTok-only and scheduling-centric;** we deliberately skip social scheduling in V1 and go multi-format/multi-destination on output — position OpenShorts as the *creation/editing* tool, not a posting farm.
  - **Editing is preset-only and assets are stock-y.** Our edge is **editable text directly on AI-generated slideshows/reels (no export to CapCut)** and branded b-roll — beat them on creative control and "doesn't look stock."
  - **No "paste a viral link → replicate the format"** and **no campaign-variation batch.** Both are explicit OpenShorts bets — build them as the headline differentiators ReelFarm lacks.
  - **Custom avatars are gated/capped behind paid tiers.** A more generous avatar/character experience (especially in onboarding) is a sharp wedge.

## UX teardown — live dashboard inspection (July 2026)

First-hand notes from inspecting reel.farm/dashboard (logged-in session), used as the
blueprint for ClipZoo's Characters + Hook+Demo avatar features:

**Create UGC ads (`/dashboard/ugc`)** — strict 3-step vertical form:
1. **Hook** — carousel of ~30 pre-written hooks with arrows (e.g. "um my nutritionist
   friend just told me that food tracking apps are literally WASTING our time wtf").
2. **AI avatar** — 3×10 grid of selfie-style avatar *video* thumbnails, ~8 pages (~240
   defaults), tabs: **Default / My UGC / Uploads**.
3. **Demos** — "None" + "+" thumbnails to attach product demo videos.
Right side: live 9:16 preview — the selected avatar clip plays with the hook text burned
on top (white text, black outline, ~3 wrapped lines), with **three text-position toggles**
(top/center/bottom) and a **Sound** picker. Generate gated by subscription.
Below: "My Videos" gallery grid with pagination.

**AI Characters (`/dashboard/characters`)** — the "one character, many looks" system:
- Left panel: character list + **New Character**.
- **Edit Character**: attribute-based identity editor — Identity (gender cards, age-range
  chips), Ethnicity, Skin Details, Face Shape, Face Details, Hair, Eyes & Brows,
  Nose & Ears, Body, Style & Accessories, Marks & Features — plus **Randomize**,
  "Import Prompt", "Copy Attributes JSON", portrait preview with "Re-render Preview",
  name field, Save Changes.
- **Character detail**: gallery of generated images + bottom prompt bar — *"Describe a
  scene, pose, outfit… or click Randomize"* with a **Random** button and a model selector
  showing **Nano Banana Pro** (Google's Gemini image family) — their look generation is
  Gemini-based image editing with the character as reference.

**What ClipZoo copied (shipped 2026-07-02):** attribute chips → Gemini portrait →
per-character looks via reference-image prompting; avatar step (None / My characters /
Upload) in Hook+Demo; outlined text style + position toggles; characters reusable across
hook demos, slideshows, and AI actor ads.
