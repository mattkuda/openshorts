## What they do

Arcads is an AI UGC ad generator. You write (or upload) a short script, pick a realistic AI "actor" from a large library, and Arcads renders a vertical talking-head video of that actor delivering your script with synced lip movement, facial expressions, and natural gestures — typically in about two minutes. It is purpose-built for performance marketers running paid social (TikTok / Meta / YouTube Shorts) who need a high volume of UGC-style creative to test.

The product's whole reason for being is volume and speed: instead of paying a human UGC creator $80–$200+ per clip and waiting days, you generate many actor/script/hook permutations in minutes for roughly $11 each. It supports text-to-speech and speech-to-speech (upload or record your own audio and transfer the delivery onto the chosen actor), plus translation/localization into 30+ languages.

Arcads markets a library variously reported as "300+" (most hands-on reviews) up to "1,000+" actors (the official site and higher tiers, which also add custom/cloned avatars). The actors span demographics and settings, and you filter to match your brand. The official site also now lists adjacent AI tools — caption generation, video translation, actor swapping, background removal, video extension — but in practice the core, reliable output is still the raw spoken clip, and reviewers consistently describe Arcads as "a generator, not an editor."

It is positioned as the clean, focused core of an AI UGC stack: it nails the performance (the actor saying the words convincingly) and deliberately leaves heavy post-production to other tools.

## Core workflow

1. **Create a project** and choose the "Talking Actor" / UGC ad type.
2. **Script editor** — type your script directly (≈400 character / short-ad length), or upload/record audio for speech-to-speech. Optional automatic emotion/tone variation.
3. **Actor selection** — open the actor library and use left-rail **filters: gender, age range, ethnicity/skin tone, and setting (home / office / car / outdoor)** to narrow to a match.
4. **Free voice preview** — click any actor to hear a ~10-second sample of *your* script in that actor's voice. This costs **no credits** — you audition delivery before committing.
5. **Add one or many actors** — attach the same script to multiple actors (and vary hooks/CTAs) to fan out variations in a single run.
6. **Generate** — click Generate; render takes ~2 min per clip. Review, then download the MP4 (or regenerate).
7. **Export to an editor** — to add captions, B-roll, on-screen hooks/CTAs, music, or graphics, you take the clip into CapCut / Premiere (Arcads does not assemble the finished ad).

## Key features

- Large AI-actor library (reported 300+ to 1,000+) with realistic lip-sync, expressions, and gestures.
- Actor filters: gender, age range, ethnicity/skin tone, and setting (home/office/car/outdoor).
- **Free voice preview** of your script per actor, before any credit is spent.
- Text-to-speech and speech-to-speech (upload/record audio, transfer your delivery onto an AI actor).
- Bulk / multi-actor generation: one script → many actors/variations in a single operation for A/B testing hooks, angles, CTAs, and personas (marketed as generating "10–30 variants per campaign week").
- "Workflow" feature: recreate an existing video or a set of B-roll scenes with different actors in one click.
- Translation/localization into 30+ languages; custom/cloned avatars on higher tiers; API access on Pro.
- Adjacent AI tools listed on site: caption generation, actor swapping, background removal, video extension, frame extraction, upscaling.

## Output / content types

- Vertical (9:16) MP4 talking-head UGC ad clips, optimized for TikTok / Reels / Shorts.
- Single-actor spokesperson clips; multi-actor batches of the same script.
- Translated/dubbed versions of a clip in 30+ languages.
- Raw clip only — no fully-composited ad (no burned-in hooks/CTAs, B-roll cutaways, or motion graphics from the core flow); actors are speaking-to-camera and cannot hold or demo a physical product.

## Strengths

- **Cleanest, fastest core loop in the category:** script → actor → render in ~2 minutes, with a genuinely simple 3-step UX.
- **Free voice preview before spending credits** (the standout UX detail): clicking an actor plays ~10 sec of *your* script in that voice for free. Reviewers explicitly note "you don't spend your credits until after you've reviewed the actor's voice, so you won't accidentally waste credits on voices you don't like." This is preview-before-spend done right and removes the biggest anxiety of a credit-metered tool.
- **Built for volume / A/B testing:** multi-actor + hook/CTA fan-out makes it easy to spin up many creative variants per campaign — ideal for paid-social testing.
- **Realism:** avatars, lip-sync, and emotion control are consistently praised as convincing for UGC.
- **Large, well-filtered actor library** and 30+ language localization for global campaigns.

## Weaknesses & gaps

- **Raw clip only — must export to an external editor.** Arcads is "a generator, not an editor." To add captions, B-roll, on-screen hooks, CTAs, music, or graphics you must download and finish the clip in CapCut/Premiere. This is the central gap.
- **No free trial; opaque, pricey entry.** Pricing isn't on the public site (appears only behind sign-up), there's no free tier, and the minimum commitment is ~$110/mo for just 10 videos. Credits on lower tiers reportedly don't roll over.
- **Credit pressure undercuts the testing pitch:** the product is sold on volume A/B testing, yet 10–20 videos/mo on entry tiers makes heavy testing expensive.
- **Speaking-to-camera only:** actors can't hold, display, or interact with a physical product, limiting e-commerce/product-demo use.
- **Library figure is inconsistent** across sources (300+ vs 1,000+), suggesting the larger number may be gated to higher tiers.

## Relevant to OpenShorts (steal / avoid)

- **Steal:**
  - **Free preview-before-spend.** Let users audition the expensive/credit-burning step (voice, actor, hook) for free before committing a render. This single UX detail is Arcads' most-praised feature and directly lowers the anxiety of a credit/usage model.
  - **CSV / batch as "campaign mode."** Treat "one input → a set of outputs" as a first-class flow: fan out variations by swapping hooks, CTAs, actors, and backgrounds in one operation. This maps cleanly onto OpenShorts' "a campaign generates a set of content," and is exactly how performance marketers think (test 10–30 variants/week).
  - Clean 3-step pipeline and tight, opinionated filters (gender, ethnicity, age, setting) instead of an overwhelming editor.
- **Avoid / their gap we exploit:**
  - **No in-app finishing.** Arcads stops at the raw talking-head and dumps users into CapCut/Premiere for captions, B-roll, hooks, and CTAs. OpenShorts attacks exactly this gap with **in-app editable text on slideshows/reels** (captions, hooks, CTAs you can edit without leaving the app) — turning "raw clip → finished ad" from a separate tool into one continuous flow.
  - Avoid the opaque, no-trial, high-entry pricing wall — let users see value (e.g., a free preview/first render) before a steep commitment.
