The copy iteration (deck #1 → deck #2)

Deck #1's renderer was right but its words were transcribed-speech mush:

"SQUAT WEAK AT BOTTOM? HERE'S WHY I THINK" · "OR LIKE, YOUR GLUTES JUST AREN'T REALLY HELPING OUT DOWN THERE?"

Fix: rewrote the generation prompt in charshow.py — poster-copy rules (banned filler words, hedge ban, fragments encouraged), a specificity quota (half the slides must contain a number or exercise name), problem→why→fix ordering, and a few-shot example deck. Deck #2 above is the result — first try, no cherry-picking.
